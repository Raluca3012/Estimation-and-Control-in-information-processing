Expectation (Mean) Analysis

The data was generated from a Uniform(0,1) distribution. 
The theoretical mean of this distribution is 0.5.
After computing the sample mean, we observed that it is close to 0.5, but not exactly equal. 
This difference is normal and is caused by the randomness of the generated sample.
As the number of samples increases, the sample mean becomes closer to the theoretical mean. 
This is explained by the Law of Large Numbers.
In conclusion, the experiment shows that the sample mean is a good estimator of the expected value.
