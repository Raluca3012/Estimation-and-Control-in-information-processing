Variance Analysis

Variance measures how spread out the values are around the mean. 
A small variance means the values are close to the mean, while a larger variance means they are more spread out.
For the Uniform(0,1) distribution, the theoretical variance is approximately 0.083. 
The computed variance is close to this value, but not exactly the same due to randomness.
We also compared two datasets with different ranges. 
The dataset with a larger interval had a higher variance, which shows that the spread of the data increases with the range.
In conclusion, variance is a useful measure to understand how much the data varies around the mean.
