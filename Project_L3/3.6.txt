Effect of Variance

When the variance is small, the values are concentrated close to the mean, so the histogram looks narrower and taller.
When the variance is larger, the values are more spread out, so the histogram becomes wider and flatter.
This shows that variance controls the spread of the distribution.
A higher variance means greater uncertainty, because the values can deviate more from the mean.
In conclusion, the variance has a strong effect on the shape of a Gaussian distribution and on the level of uncertainty in the data.
