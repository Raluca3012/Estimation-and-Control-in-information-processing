Gaussian Distribution Analysis

The histogram shows a bell-shaped curve, which is characteristic of a normal distribution. 
Most values are concentrated around the mean (0), and fewer values appear as we move away from it.
We compared this with a Uniform(0,1) distribution. 
Unlike the Gaussian distribution, the uniform distribution is flat, meaning all values have equal probability.
The Gaussian distribution shows clustering around the mean, while the uniform distribution spreads values evenly across the interval.
This comparison highlights the difference between concentrated and evenly distributed data.
