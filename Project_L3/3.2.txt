Interpretation of distribution

From the histogram, we can observe that the values are spread evenly between 0 and 1. 
The shape of the histogram is approximately flat, which is expected for a uniform distribution.
This means that every value in the interval [0,1] has roughly the same probability of occurring. 
There are small differences between the bars, but this is normal due to randomness and the limited number of samples.
If we increase the number of generated values, the histogram would become more uniform and closer to the ideal theoretical shape.
